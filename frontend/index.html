About VendorIntel

What is VendorIntel?

VendorIntel is a vendor reliability intelligence platform that helps organizations manage, evaluate, and monitor their vendors in one place. By tracking performance metrics and flagging risk early, it helps procurement teams choose reliable suppliers and keep their supply chain resilient.
