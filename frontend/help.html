Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common platform questions.

The dashboard provides a role-specific overview of the activities and risks relevant to your account.

Use the KPI cards, charts, alerts, recent activity, and quick-access actions to monitor your responsibilities.

Authorized users can view or manage vendors according to their assigned role.

Vendor records may include contact information, category, reliability score, compliance score, and operational status.

Purchase orders connect procurement requirements with a selected vendor.

Depending on your role, you can create, review, update, or monitor purchase orders and their status.

Delivery records contain expected and actual delivery information.

Delays, damaged goods, and delivery status can be monitored by users who have the appropriate permissions.

Use the Contracts module to view contract numbers, vendors, dates, values, terms, and contract status.

The Invoices module is used to monitor invoice information, amounts, due dates, and payment status.

Notifications are available from the Notifications module and the shared navigation interface when enabled for your role.

Module visibility is controlled by your assigned role and permissions.

If a module is not available to your account, contact an authorized administrator.

Open Settings from the shared navigation. In the Security section, enter your current password and your new password.
